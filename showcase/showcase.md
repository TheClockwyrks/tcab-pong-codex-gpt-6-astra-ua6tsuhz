# Carom

Two paddles. One ball. Every angle is an opportunity. Carom is a quick arcade duel
on a dark court, where a clean return can become a curling shot and a pair of
turning obstacles can change the rally in an instant.

The opening serve gives you a moment to find your position. Meet the ball head-on
for a straight return, catch it near the edge of your paddle for a sharper angle,
or slide through contact to put spin into its flight. Follow the mint trail as
your shot bends across the court.

Those amber bars are more than things to avoid. They sway and rotate, opening
lanes and offering new bank shots. A return that looks safe can bounce back at an
unexpected angle. Read the court, hold your ground, and make your next touch count.

Take on the machine in Solo or challenge a friend on the same keyboard in Versus.
Race to eleven, but keep your nerve at match point: you need a two-point lead to
finish it. There is always room for one more rally.
